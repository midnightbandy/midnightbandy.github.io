ZipPG

India's verified search engine for PGs, hostels, and co-living spaces — starting with Pune's Hinjewadi–Kharadi–Wakad–Magarpatta corridor.

Next.jsPostgres · NeonPrismaLeaflet

The problem

PG listings in most Indian cities are scraped, stale, or brokered — no way for a tenant to know a listing is real before showing up. ZipPG's answer: every listing is owner-KYC checked and photo/video verified before it goes live. No fake rooms, no broker markup.

ZipScore

The actual product differentiation isn't the listings — it's a computed 0–100 trust score built from real platform activity: verification recency, verified-stay review quality, and price fairness against the local market. A scraper-based competitor can list the same address; they can't fabricate the activity a real score is built from.

How listings actually get onboarded

Deliberately not scraping. A field-agent CRM turns a CSV import into a real prospect pipeline for manually onboarding PG owners who exist but aren't online yet — the actual answer to "how do you get real listings without scraping," decided early and held to since.

Where it stands

Auth, listing CRUD, the verification console, in-app messaging, map-based search, and the ZipScore engine are all real and covered by automated tests — no mocks. Migrated from a local SQLite MVP to Postgres to get ready for a real deploy.

Honest state: no live production deployment yet — that's the current, explicit priority, not an unknown. Photo storage is local-disk today, a known pre-launch gap, not a surprise.
